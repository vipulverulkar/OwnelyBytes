## 📱 Detailed Screen Capabilities & Features

### 1. 🏠 Home Dashboard
![Home Dashboard](/home/vipul/.cache/Google/AndroidStudio2026.2.2/projects/ovenlybytes.b89e295f/.artifacts/51723015-b9bf-4b91-86e8-7b2dc40202ec/01_dashboard.png)
- Operational KPIs: Active Orders, Due Today, Total Revenue (₹), Pending Balance.
- High-priority Low Stock Warning Alert banner.
- "Take New Order" FAB and simplified 5-tab bottom navigation.

### 2. 📜 Orders List & Channel Tracking
![Orders List Tracking](/home/vipul/.cache/Google/AndroidStudio2026.2.2/projects/ovenlybytes.b89e295f/.artifacts/51723015-b9bf-4b91-86e8-7b2dc40202ec/02_orders.png)
- Color badges for order channels: **WhatsApp (🟢)**, **Phone Call (🔵)**, **Walk-in (🟣)**.
- Order status filters: Received, Baking, Ready for Pickup, Delivered.
- Quick 1-tap WhatsApp receipt generator and native phone call dialer.

### 3. 📊 Financial Reports & Revenue Analytics
![Financial Reports](/home/vipul/.cache/Google/AndroidStudio2026.2.2/projects/ovenlybytes.b89e295f/.artifacts/51723015-b9bf-4b91-86e8-7b2dc40202ec/03_reports.png)
- Time period filters: **All Time**, **Today**, **This Month**, **This Year**.
- Daily, Monthly, and Annual revenue breakdown trends.
- Outstanding payment directory with 1-tap WhatsApp balance reminders.

### 4. 🎂 Menu Catalog & Item Badges
![Menu Catalog](/home/vipul/.cache/Google/AndroidStudio2026.2.2/projects/ovenlybytes.b89e295f/.artifacts/51723015-b9bf-4b91-86e8-7b2dc40202ec/04_menu.png)
- Product categories: Classic Cakes, Premium Collection, Brownies, Tea Cakes, Cookies.
- Visual item category icons, multi-tier portion prices, and availability toggles.

### 5. 📝 Order Form & Paginated Saved Clients
![New Order Form](/home/vipul/.cache/Google/AndroidStudio2026.2.2/projects/ovenlybytes.b89e295f/.artifacts/51723015-b9bf-4b91-86e8-7b2dc40202ec/06_new_order.png)
![Paginated Saved Client Picker](/home/vipul/.cache/Google/AndroidStudio2026.2.2/projects/ovenlybytes.b89e295f/.artifacts/51723015-b9bf-4b91-86e8-7b2dc40202ec/07_saved_clients.png)
- Channel selection, searchable and paginated saved customer picker (5 clients/page).
- Item selection picker with realistic category icons, cake message piping text, customization charges, and balance calculations.

### 6. ⚙️ Settings, Themes & Backup Hub
![Settings & Backup Hub](/home/vipul/.cache/Google/AndroidStudio2026.2.2/projects/ovenlybytes.b89e295f/.artifacts/51723015-b9bf-4b91-86e8-7b2dc40202ec/05_settings.png)
- Management hub shortcuts for Client CRM and Bakery Stock.
- 8 Bakery Color Themes: Artisan Cocoa, Strawberry Shortcake, Honey Vanilla, Pistachio Matcha, Lemon Chiffon, Blueberry Macaron, Peach Cobbler, Dark Mocha.
- Multi-format backup & restore (**SQL .sql**, **CSV .csv**, **JSON .json**).
